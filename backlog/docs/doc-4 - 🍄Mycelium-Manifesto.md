## Background / 背景

**EN:** The rapid advance of AI is disrupting every traditional organization built around capital accumulation—and forcing us to ask a fundamental question: **what is irreducibly human?** Our answer: **imagination, creativity, and emotion**. These are not features to be automated away—they are the singular core of every individual, the things no model can replace.

At the same time, we observe a direction: people are leaving large organizations for communities, moving from passive employment toward self-directed exploration. We believe **Loose Communities and Extreme Individuals**—not corporations—will become the defining organizational form of the future.

This disruption and this direction together are giving rise to a societal shift we call the **Meaning Economy**: using economic activity not as an end in itself, but as a medium for exploring who we are and what truly matters to us. This shift may be forced upon us, or consciously chosen—but it is all but inevitable. (See [Course 0](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1JurLiAwfaHeNdXaYZP_CdMltLfhCfn210Ebgk5o9a0g/edit?usp=sharing))

---

**CN:** AI 的快速发展，正在冲击一切以资本增殖为核心的传统组织形态，也迫使我们重新追问：**人类个体不可替代的内核是什么？** 我们的答案是：**想象力、创造力与情感**——这是任何模型都无法替代的东西。

与此同时，我们观察到一个方向：人们正在从大组织流向社区，从被动雇佣走向自主探索。我们认为，**松散社区与极致个体**将成为未来最重要的组织形态——而非公司。

这股冲击与这个方向的汇聚，正在催生一场影响全社会的范式转变——我们称之为**意义经济**：把经济活动不作为目的本身，而作为探索"我是谁""什么对我真正重要"的媒介。这场转变或许是被迫，或许是主动，但几乎不可避免。（参见 [Course 0](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1JurLiAwfaHeNdXaYZP_CdMltLfhCfn210Ebgk5o9a0g/edit?usp=sharing)）

## Our Exploration: The Meaning Economy / 我们的探索方式：意义经济

**EN:** What sets the Meaning Economy apart from conventional economies?

Capital-driven organizations—companies, funds, growth machines—treat economic expansion as the singular goal. The individual exists to serve the machine: independent thought, imagination, and emotional expression are liabilities, not assets. The person is not the purpose; growth is.

The Meaning Economy uses economics differently: as an instrument of exploration. It allows—even requires—individual imagination, creativity, and emotional expression. Any economic model that honors these human qualities is part of the Meaning Economy. It is not anti-commerce; it is a reclamation of what commerce can become when humans are the purpose, not the product.

Communities are the natural home of the Meaning Economy. Unlike capital-driven organizations where the individual is not permitted independent thought, communities explore rather than extract—they cohere around shared meaning, and they treat each person's inner world as something worth building infrastructure for.

---

**CN:** 意义经济与其他经济形态的本质区别是什么？

资本驱动的组织——公司、基金、增殖机器——将经济扩张本身视为唯一目标。个体服务于机器：独立思考、想象力与情感表达在这里是负担，不是资产。个人不是目的，增长才是。

意义经济以不同的方式使用经济：把它作为探索的工具。它允许、甚至要求个体的想象力、创造力与情感表达。凡是尊重这些人类特质的经济模式，我们认为都属于意义经济。意义经济并非反商业——而是一次重新定义：当人本身成为目的而非产品时，商业可以成为什么。

社区是意义经济天然的栖居地。不同于资本驱动的组织不允许个体有任何独立思考，社区以探索代替攫取，以共同的意义而非共同的利润凝聚，把每一个个体的内心世界——他的想象力、创造力与情感——视为值得为之构建基础设施的东西。

## Vision / 愿景

**EN:** Empowering **Extreme Individuals** to thrive within diverse **Loose Communities** connected by the **Mycelium Protocol**—reclaiming their imagination, creativity, and emotions to transcend alienation, find meaning, and truly "become themselves."

**CN:** 极致个体生活在由 **Mycelium（菌丝协议）** 连接的多个松散社区中，释放想象力、创造力与情感，摆脱异化，寻找意义，回归自我。

## Mission / 使命

**EN:** To realize our vision by consistently building **Digital Public Goods**, fostering a **Sustainable Mycelial Network** for collaboration, and exploring innovation through **Prediction Markets**—providing the infrastructure for the Meaning Economy to flourish.

**CN:** 通过持续构建数字公共物品，形成可持续协作的菌丝网络，以预测市场的方式探索创新——为意义经济的繁荣提供基础设施，从而达成愿景。
